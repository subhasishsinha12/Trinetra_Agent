# EyeCard

EyeCard presents one of the three stages of the engine (Pratyaksha, Ganita, Viveka) with its numbered checks.

**Use** the three together, in order, in a row of equal columns (stacked under 900px). Each card takes its eye colour on its top rule, numeral and kicker. Nothing else in it is coloured.

**Consumer provides**
- `eye`: `1` perceive (`eye-perceive`), `2` measure (`eye-measure`), `3` discern (`eye-discern`). The kicker text is generated.
- `title`: the stage's job in sentence case ("Intake and classification").
- `steps`: three to five short checks, each citing its paragraph where one applies.

**Don't** reuse eye colours for status, or show a single eye card on its own.
